**[R] Retatrutide nausea management (Mar 5)**
- Claude AI conversation titled "Managing nausea on retatrutide"
- Retatrutide is a triple GLP-1/GIP/glucagon receptor agonist (weight loss / metabolic medication, weekly injection)
- Common GI side effects: nausea, sulfur burps ("wet burps"), eructation
- Advice: eat smaller/more frequent meals, avoid fatty/fried foods, avoid carbonated drinks, reduce high-sulfur foods (eggs, cruciferous vegetables, red meat), short walk after meals
- OTC remedies: ginger (tea/chews/capsules), peppermint tea, famotidine (Pepcid), simethicone (Gas-X)
- Nausea peaks 2–3 days after injection (consistent with GLP-1 peak plasma concentration)
- Dose titration: stay at current dose longer before increasing, or step back temporarily
- Prescription option: ondansetron (Zofran) if OTC insufficient
