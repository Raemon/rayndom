# History & Research Rules

## Tactical Research Goals

- Get an accurate and complete history of the most important parts of the transformer evolution (from perceptron through today's best models)

- Make sure that history is reasonably concise, even in it's full, expanded form.

- Present information about that in an even more abbreviated overview form that conveys the most important details, with an expanded mode for the full details

## When updating history or technical entries

- Don't just fix the current problem. Look at the entire history item, and ask if it's still reasonably concise, focused on most important high level details.

- Follow up with a thorough "does this detail make sense given all of my knowledge about ML? Would senior ML researchers or historians agree with how I was conceptualizing this?" (don't just check for simple fact-checks, check if how you are choosing to conceptualize this is accurate.)
